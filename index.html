Home

Welcome to the home of Everjoy Studios! We are a one man team focusing on Linux first games in a wide array of genres. Our first game is an Idle Clicker game called T.I.L.L.I.E.

You'll play as an assistant to TILLIE who is an AI in charge of managing and running a mining facility on Xephina-7. Due to an unforseen decompression, she is left without any workers, so she reaches out across communication channels looking for help to complete her mission.

Solve the mystery of what happened to the last crew, harvest precious Dirunien for the UAGF (United Armed Galactic Front).
